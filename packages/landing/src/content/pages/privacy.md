title: Privacy Policy
---

This privacy policy describes how Challenger Fantasy ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our services, including our mobile application and website located at https://challengerfantasy.com (collectively, the "services").

## 1. Information we collect

We collect:

- Account information: email address, username, and optional profile details (e.g., profile picture).
- Usage information: screen views, interactions, and other analytics data via PostHog, which may collect device, operating system, and interaction details.
- Error information: technical error data via Sentry.
- Preferences: settings such as display mode (light/dark) stored locally on your device.
- Contest and game data: your picks, draft selections, scores, and contest history generated through use of the services.

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:
- operate and maintain the services;
- manage your account and fantasy contests;
- analyze usage and improve features;
- diagnose and fix technical issues;
- protect the security and integrity of the services.

## 3. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:
- with service providers who process data on our behalf, including Supabase (database and authentication) and PostHog (analytics);
- as required by law or legal process;
- to protect our rights, safety, or property, or that of our users or the public.

## 4. Data storage and security

Your account data is stored securely using Supabase. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Local storage and device storage

We use local device storage to store authentication tokens and user preferences. Third-party services used by the services (such as analytics) may use similar technologies.

## 6. Data retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email address below.

## 7. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to restrict or object to certain processing activities. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. If changes are significant, we will post a notice within the services. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy or wish to request deletion of your data, contact us at contact@challengerfantasy.com.
