---
title: Challenger Fantasy
---

Challenger Fantasy exists because MMA deserves a great fantasy game. Built from the ground up by longtime fans of the sport, it's designed to make every fight fun.

The game is simple: two or more players draft fighters from an upcoming card, then watch the points roll in based on finishes, strikes landed, and more. Different game modes offer different scoring metrics, which require different approaches. The draft brings a PvP element to MMA that doesn't exist elsewhere, and the multi-dimensional scoring means there's always an angle to play, a value pick to find, or a matchup to exploit.

We're just getting started. More stats, deeper fighter data, and community features are on the way. The best way to support Challenger Fantasy is to use it, tell your friends, and let us know what you think. Every session, share, and piece of feedback helps shape what it becomes.

We hope you enjoy.
